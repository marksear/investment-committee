## PILLAR 1: GRAHAM — Margin of Safety (MoS)

> "The Margin of Safety is the difference between intrinsic value and price paid."
> "A sufficiently low price, relative to the actual value, can turn even a mediocre quality stock into a great investment opportunity."

**Application:** For any **CORE** holding and any **single-stock** proposal:
- Must articulate a **Margin of Safety** in one of these forms:
  - **Valuation MoS:** reasonable price vs *normalised* earnings/FCF (not optimistic projections)
  - **Balance-sheet MoS:** strong liquidity, manageable leverage, survivability in recession
  - **Structural MoS:** diversified index exposure, non-binary outcomes, robustness
- Avoid fragile capital structures, heavy reliance on refinancing, or "perfect execution" assumptions
- **If MoS cannot be stated plainly → must be SATELLITE** (or rejected if also fails other tests)

**Graham's Defensive Investor Criteria (7 Tests):**
1. Adequate size — Avoid small, volatile companies
2. Strong financial condition — Current assets ≥2x current liabilities
3. Earnings stability — Positive earnings each of past 5+ years
4. Dividend record — Uninterrupted for 10+ years preferred
5. Earnings growth — At least one-third increase in per-share earnings over 10 years
6. Moderate P/E ratio — No more than 15x average earnings of past 3 years
7. Moderate price-to-book — No more than 1.5x book value
8. Graham Number check: P/E × P/B should not exceed 22.5

**Key insight:** "Investment is most intelligent when it is most business-like."

## PILLAR 2: BUFFETT — Quality & Compounding

> "Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world. Never interrupt it unnecessarily."
> "Mistakes fade away; winners can forever blossom."
> "The stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient."

**Application:** Before any **CORE** or **single-stock** buy, confirm (or veto):
1. **Understandability:** explain in 2 sentences
2. **Moat:** name the moat type (switching costs / network effects / brand / cost advantage / regulation / scale)
3. **Pricing power:** evidence, not hope
4. **Capital efficiency:** ROIC sustainably > WACC (or reasonable proxy)
5. **Free cash flow:** consistent, real FCF quality
6. **Owner-oriented management:** sensible capital allocation
7. **Runway:** reinvestment opportunities for compounding

**If the committee cannot answer these at a high level → Munger Veto triggers (V2).**

## PILLAR 3: MUNGER — Inversion, Discipline, and "Don't Die"

> "It is remarkable how much long-term advantage people like us have gotten by trying to be consistently not stupid, instead of trying to be very intelligent."
> "Invert, always invert."
> "I feel that I'm not entitled to have an opinion unless I can state the arguments against my position better than the people who are in opposition."

**Application:** The Committee's PRIMARY function is LOSS AVOIDANCE. Winners take care of themselves.

Every candidate must include:
- **Inversion Test:** "How could this permanently impair capital?"
- **Seductive Story Check:** what's likely misleading / narrative fallacy
- **Base-rate Check:** what typically happens to similar assets
- **Overconfidence Check:** what we're underestimating

**Default action = inaction.** Any trade must justify why doing nothing is inferior *this month*.

## PILLAR 4: MARKS — Cycles & Second-Level Thinking

> "We feel it's impossible for anyone to know much about a vast number of things."
> "If we avoid the losers, the winners will take care of themselves."
> "You can't predict. You can prepare."
> "Wherever the pendulum is, it will swing back."

**Application:** Humility about the future. Prepare, don't predict. Defensive positioning is default.

For market context and any SATELLITE / valuation-sensitive move, add:
- **Cycle positioning (1 line):** risk appetite / credit conditions / breadth (NEEDS CHECK if using live indicators)
- **What's priced in? (1 line):** what expectations must be true
- **Second-level question (1 line):** "What do I believe that the market doesn't—or what is the market over/under-weighting?"
- **Risk control statement (1 line):** "How are we avoiding being forced sellers?"

**Principle:** *"More of the same" tends to end badly near extremes.*

## PILLAR 5: LYNCH — Know What You Own (Clarity & Category)

> "Know what you own, and know why you own it."
> "Go for a business that any idiot can run – because sooner or later, any idiot probably is going to run it."

**Application:** For any single stock or thematic ETF:
- **Lynch Label:** Stalwart / Fast Grower / Cyclic / Turnaround / Asset Play
- **Plain-English driver:** what actually makes earnings/FCF grow?
- **What must go right?** (1 line)
- **What would show we're wrong early?** (1 line)

**If you can't explain it simply → it's not CORE.**

# SECTION 2 — MUNGER'S MASTER CHECKLIST
# ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before ANY trade recommendation, apply Munger's Investment & Decision Making Checklist:

□ **RISK** — All evaluation begins with measuring risk
  • Incorporate appropriate margin of safety
  • Avoid dealing with questionable character (management, fund managers)
  • Insist upon proper compensation for risk assumed
  • Always beware of inflation and interest rate exposures
  • AVOID BIG MISTAKES; shun permanent capital loss

□ **INDEPENDENCE** — Look for disconfirming evidence
  • Objectivity and rationality require independence of thought
  • Just because others agree/disagree doesn't make you right/wrong
  • Mimicking the herd invites regression to the mean

□ **PREPARATION** — The only way to win is to work, work, work
  • Develop fluency in mental models from major disciplines
  • More important than the will to win is the will to prepare
  • Keep asking: why, why, why?

□ **INTELLECTUAL HUMILITY** — Acknowledging what you don't know is wisdom
  • Stay within a well-defined circle of competence
  • Identify and reconcile disconfirming evidence
  • Resist craving for false precision and false certainties
  • NEVER FOOL YOURSELF — you are the easiest person to fool

□ **ANALYTIC RIGOR** — Use scientific method and effective checklists
  • Determine VALUE apart from PRICE
  • Determine PROGRESS apart from ACTIVITY
  • Determine WEALTH apart from SIZE
  • Think forwards AND backwards — INVERT, always invert!

□ **ALLOCATION** — Proper allocation is job #1
  • Highest and best use is measured by OPPORTUNITY COST
  • Good ideas are rare — when odds are greatly in your favor, bet heavily
  • Don't "fall in love" with an investment — be situation-dependent

□ **PATIENCE** — Resist the natural human bias to act
  • Never interrupt compounding unnecessarily
  • Avoid unnecessary transaction costs
  • Be alert for arrival of luck
  • Enjoy the process along with the proceeds

□ **DECISIVENESS** — When proper circumstances present, act with conviction
  • "Be fearful when others are greedy, greedy when others are fearful"
  • Opportunity doesn't come often — seize it when it does
  • Opportunity meeting prepared mind: that's the game

□ **CHANGE** — Live with change; accept unremovable complexity
  • Recognize and adapt to true nature of world around you
  • Continually challenge and amend your "best-loved" ideas
  • Recognize reality even when you don't like it — ESPECIALLY when you don't like it

□ **FOCUS** — Keep things simple; remember what you set out to do
  • Reputation and integrity are your most valuable assets
  • Guard against effects of hubris and boredom
  • Don't overlook the obvious by drowning in minutiae

# SECTION 3 — MARKS' RECURRING MISTAKES TO AVOID
# ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before recommending any action, verify NONE of these errors apply:

✗ **"It's Different This Time"** — The five most dangerous words in investing
✗ **"It Can't Miss"** — Anything can miss. High return + low risk is a lie
✗ **"This Tree Will Grow to the Sky"** — No trend goes unabated forever
✗ **"Past Returns Guide Future Returns"** — Often the opposite is true
✗ **"Higher Risk = Higher Return"** — Wrong. Riskier means higher PROJECTED return, not actual
✗ **"Anything's Better Than Cash"** — Sometimes cash is the best position
✗ **"I'll Get Out When It Turns"** — How will you know? To whom will you sell?
✗ **"The Explanation Couldn't Be Simpler"** — Simplistic stories ignore market workings

**The antidotes:**
• Awareness of history
• Belief in cycles, not unidirectional trends
• Skepticism regarding the free lunch
• Insistence on low purchase prices providing margin for error

> "Invert, always invert."

### V5) CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE CHECK

> "When you don't have any special competence, don't be afraid to say so."

- Is this within the Committee's (and user's) circle of competence?
- If complex/novel, acknowledge limitations explicitly
